## 2. Authority classes and approval labels

Every expected result has one label:

- `TECHNICAL`: its oracle follows frozen schema, lifecycle, time, identity,
  ordering or aggregation mechanics and needs no toxicological judgement;
- `SCIENTIFIC-REVIEW-REQUIRED`: its oracle depends on the candidate QPS
  channel, endpoint, population or dependency policy;
- `SCIENTIFIC-APPROVED`: reserved for an independently approved oracle. No case
  currently has this label.

`TECHNICAL` does not mean that the candidate policy is publishable. A technical
case may validate an interpreter only after the surrounding policy fixture is
accepted as a test input.

## 4. Decision shorthand

The tables use:

```text
I = included / resolved / contributing
X = excluded / resolved / none
D = excluded / deferred / none
```

Unless a case specifies otherwise, `secondary_reasons=[]`. Rule references are
ordered by the candidate policy evaluation plan. A blocked case lists the
primary deciding rule; reachable earlier passing rules remain in the canonical
trace and later rules are `not_evaluated`.

For every decision, `ordered_rule_refs` is exactly the complete 18-rule
`evaluation_plan` from the candidate JSON. The corresponding trace event for a
later unreachable rule is present with `outcome=not_evaluated`; therefore the
ordered rule-reference oracle never depends on short-circuit iteration.

Manifest summaries use:

```text
(included_count, excluded_count, deferred_count, selection_state)
```

## 6. Secondary-reason oracle

oracle is:

  namespace: wye_selection_core

This case freezes multiple reachable failures, unique primary selection and
ordered secondary retention without invoking scientific applicability. A later
additional multi-scientific-failure case may be reviewed, but is not needed to
validate the structural precedence mechanism.

## 7. Trace oracle

For every case the trace contains one event per registered rule:

- reachable passing gates: `outcome=pass`;
- deciding exclusion: `outcome=block` and its primary reason;
- deciding unresolved gate: `outcome=defer` and its primary reason;
- every later rule after a block/defer: `outcome=not_evaluated`.

Events are ordered by `(stage_ordinal, precedence_ordinal, rule_id UTF-8)` and
use only the rule IDs and field references in the candidate policy. No source
payload dump or prose belongs in the oracle.

## 9. Execution gate

The corpus may be used to review the candidate policy. It may not authorize a
production selector until:

1. the scientific reviewer approves every `SCIENTIFIC-REVIEW-REQUIRED` case
   against the exact policy digest;
2. the validation owner independently approves expected decisions and adds the
   multi-failure precedence case;
3. the release approver records publication authorization;
4. the policy is embedded in an immutable published protocol definition.

Until then a future technical conformance interpreter may only run synthetic
policy fixtures in a non-production harness. It may not publish selection,
result, trace or execution records and may not be presented as a scientific
selector.
